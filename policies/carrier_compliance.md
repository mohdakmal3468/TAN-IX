# Commercial Freight Carrier Compliance & Standards
**Document ID:** POL-CMP-2026-05  
**Authority:** Uttar Pradesh State Transport Authority (UPSTA)  
**Applicability:** Registered Commercial Carriers Operating in the Leather Corridor

---

## 1. Carrier Registration & Minimum Eligibility
Commercial freight carriers contracted to haul leather goods between Kanpur, Unnao, Agra, and Noida must maintain verified compliance across four operational pillars:
1. **On-Time Reliability Threshold:** Must maintain a minimum rolling 30-day **on-time delivery reliability rating of 75.0%** across highway corridors. Carriers dropping below 75% are automatically deprioritized by the Logistics Agent.
2. **GPS Telematics Integration:** All dispatched vehicles must transmit real-time GPS telemetry pings at least once every **10 minutes** to the state tracking server.
3. **Goods-in-Transit Insurance:** Active comprehensive cargo transit insurance covering up to ₹25,00,000 INR per commercial consignment against water damage, highway theft, or accidental loss.

---

## 2. Vehicle Classification & Cargo Standards
- **Finished Footwear & Garments (Agra $\leftrightarrow$ Noida):** Must be hauled in closed containerized trucks or weatherproof tarpaulin-enclosed light/medium commercial vehicles (e.g. *Tata 407* or *Eicher Pro*).
- **Raw Salted Buffalo Hides & Wet-Blue (Kanpur $\leftrightarrow$ Unnao):** Moisture-sealed freight beds with zero liquid effluent leakage onto highway surfaces. Any effluent dripping onto public roadways incurs a mandatory ₹25,000 UPPCB statutory penalty.
- **Tanning Extracts & Solvents:** Must be transported in labeled industrial tankers complying with Motor Vehicles Hazmat rules.

---

## 3. Driver Safety & Duty Hour Regulations
1. **Maximum Continuous Driving:** Drivers must not exceed 4.5 hours of continuous operation without a mandatory 30-minute rest halt at designated highway rest areas.
2. **Shift Maximum:** Total driving hours must not exceed 9.0 hours within any rolling 24-hour cycle.
3. **Breathalyzer & Fit-to-Drive:** Zero-tolerance alcohol and substance policy verified by automated biometric kiosks at industrial logistics hubs before exit gate clearance.
