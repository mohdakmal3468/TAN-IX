# Emergency Freight & Stock Rebalancing Policy
**Document ID:** POL-EMF-2026-03  
**Authority:** Uttar Pradesh MSME & Export Promotion Department (ODOP Directorate)  
**Applicability:** State Leather Corridors (Kanpur, Unnao, Agra, Noida)

---

## 1. Emergency Freight Trigger Conditions
Emergency freight protocols are triggered autonomously when any district warehouse experiences:
1. **Critical Inventory Deficit:** Stock level falls below 40% of designated reorder point (e.g. Agra belt stock $\le$ 480 units against 1,200 reorder point).
2. **Production Stoppage Runway:** Available inventory runway falls below **2.5 operating days** based on the 7-day average daily burn rate.
3. **Disaster Disruptions:** An active flood warning, environmental closure, or highway blockade severing primary raw material pipelines.

---

## 2. Autonomous Carrier Selection & Green Corridor Routing
When an emergency is declared:
- **Expedited Carrier Override:** The Intra-State Logistics Agent is authorized to bypass standard lowest-cost carrier requirements and assign high-speed, high-reliability commercial carriers (e.g. *Yamuna Fast Haulers* or *UP State Freight Express*).
- **Expressway Green Corridor:** UPEIDA grants green-corridor toll priority to designated emergency transfer trucks, ensuring zero stoppage at toll plazas.
- **Detour Mandate:** If primary expressways (`LANE-03`) are disrupted, carriers must divert immediately to approved national highway alternatives (`LANE-03-ALT` via Etawah/Shikohabad) without waiting for manual operational clearance.

---

## 3. Financial Subsidies & ODOP Freight Reimbursement
1. **15% ODOP Freight Rebate:** Under the Uttar Pradesh One District One Product (ODOP) Leather Promotion Scheme, 15% of the total emergency logistics cost is subsidized by the state government for inter-district raw hide and component transfers.
2. **Fuel Surcharge Cap:** Carrier diesel fuel escalation surcharges during declared emergency transit are capped at 8% above base contract rates.
3. **Claim Processing:** Subsidized freight rebates are auto-reconciled against the state logistics ledger upon delivery sign-off in the AgentHide system.
