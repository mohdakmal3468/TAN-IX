# Delivery Service Level Agreement (SLA) by District
**Document ID:** POL-SLA-2026-01  
**Authority:** Uttar Pradesh Leather Industrial Development Board (UPLIDB)  
**Applicability:** Kanpur, Unnao, Agra, and Noida Freight Corridors

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This policy defines mandatory transit duration windows, delivery service level agreements (SLAs), and carrier demurrage penalties for commercial freight shipments moving raw hides, wet-blue crust, shoe uppers, and finished leather goods across Uttar Pradesh.

---

## 2. Standard & Priority Delivery SLAs by District Lane

### A. Agra Footwear Cluster (Destination: Agra)
- **Kanpur $\rightarrow$ Agra (`LANE-03` / `LANE-03-ALT`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 24.0 hours from dispatch timestamp to gate receipt at Sikandra / Hing Ki Mandi warehouses.
  - **Priority / Rush SLA:** 10.0 hours maximum via the Agra-Lucknow Expressway (`LANE-03`).
  - **Emergency Replenishment SLA:** 8.0 hours when the destination cluster reports inventory below critical reorder threshold (<2 days runway).
- **Unnao $\rightarrow$ Agra (`LANE-07`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 24.0 hours.
  - **Priority SLA:** 12.0 hours.
- **Noida $\rightarrow$ Agra (`LANE-12`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 12.0 hours via Yamuna Expressway.
  - **Priority SLA:** 6.0 hours.

### B. Kanpur Industrial Hub (Destination: Kanpur)
- **Unnao $\rightarrow$ Kanpur (`LANE-02`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 4.0 hours (Same-day rapid transit across NH-27 Ganga Bridge).
  - **Priority SLA:** 2.0 hours for wet-blue perishable hides.
- **Agra $\rightarrow$ Kanpur (`LANE-04`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 24.0 hours; Priority SLA: 12.0 hours.

### C. Noida Export Apparel Zone (Destination: Noida)
- **Agra $\rightarrow$ Noida (`LANE-11`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 12.0 hours via Yamuna Expressway.
  - **Priority SLA:** 5.0 hours for air cargo export connections at Delhi IGI / Jewar.
- **Kanpur $\rightarrow$ Noida (`LANE-05`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 28.0 hours; Priority SLA: 14.0 hours.
- **Unnao $\rightarrow$ Noida (`LANE-09`):**
  - **Standard SLA:** 28.0 hours; Priority SLA: 14.0 hours.

---

## 3. Grace Periods & Late Delivery Penalties
1. **Grace Window:** A 60-minute grace window applies before penalties accrue.
2. **Standard Penalty:** For every 1 hour of transit delay beyond the designated SLA, a 2.0% penalty is deducted from the contracted freight invoice (capped at 25% of total freight charge).
3. **Severe Default:** Consignments delayed past 12.0 hours trigger an automated carrier compliance review and a mandatory ₹5,000 demurrage credit to the receiving district warehouse.
4. **Force Majeure:** Verified highway blockades, expressway fog closures, or Ganges flood emergencies documented in the State Risk Registry suspend penalty calculations.
