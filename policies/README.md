# 📜 UP Leather Regulatory & Industrial Policies

This directory stores state policy guidelines, environmental norms, and statutory directives used by the **Vector Search Engine** and **Regional Risk Agent**:

1. **National Green Tribunal (NGT) Mandates**: Effluent discharge parameters for Jajmau (Kanpur) and Banthar (Unnao) CETPs.
2. **UPEIDA Expressway Freight Regulations**: Speed caps, toll schemes, and winter fog convoy rules along Agra-Lucknow and Yamuna Expressways.
3. **UP One District One Product (ODOP) Schemes**: Freight subsidies (15% rebate) for inter-cluster raw leather and footwear supply rebalancing.
4. **UPSDMA Ganges Basin Flood Directives**: High-water alerts at Narora and Kanpur Barrages affecting riverbank tanneries.
